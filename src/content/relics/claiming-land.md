*Upon the incorporation of the Imperial Academy at Merrow’s Reach into Imperial stewardship, its held materials were examined and documents determined to be significant were entered into the Imperial Archives. This letter was marked for its insight into the early history of the Empire’s expansion efforts, where most of the Empire’s own records had been lost.*

## The Letter

To the Wonderful Fellows at the Listener’s Symposium, in the Grand City of Merrow’s Reach, Jewel of the Northern Shore

Honored Sirs,

I have received word of your concerns, delivered by the bard Thomas of your Order, which is a choice in delivery that I found to be most interesting, regarding the allocation of both farmland and fortress in the surrounding terrain. It is my responsibility, as the humble servant of the people, and Prefect of the Empire, to educate the educated on the many errors in the information upon which the Symposium has based its findings.

Firstly, on the accusation that the land was stolen, I can confirm these allegations as false. The courageous and pioneering settlers who came before the Empire found only Visari and Khuldinai in the region, not other humans. These peoples found great misery in caring for this land, and our arrival only relieved them of a burden they did not desire. When we found them, many were dying from disease they claimed they had never seen before. Whether they angered the gods or succumbed to their own corruption, I cannot say, as I do not have divine providence in the matter.

Regardless, for miles around our settlements, the old peoples retreated. They left the southern Heartlands on their own accord, retreating to the isolated regions to the north, where they remain to this day. I have heard from people who have traveled to their valley, and am told they prefer the huge forest and dense mountains to the open plains they left behind.

And of the ones who remained? Maybe a handful. They freely put themselves under our protection. We did not force them. As our records show, they practically begged to belong to us. We, who do not take slaves, who are the very best at taking care of impoverished and suffering peoples, accepted with kindness. We gave the Khuldinai jobs in our quarries so that they might satisfy their own need to feel useful, to have purpose, and to show their gratitude toward the Empire.

The Heartlands belongs to the Empire, declared by the gods themselves, even if the people within do not yet recognize it. The Empire’s rule brings peace and stability. It is inevitable. This is not just an opinion of mine, it is written in the Temple’s teachings and in the books read by the wise, books which I can only assume that the Listeners have not yet read.

I would offer my personal copies, but I am far from home and did not bring any with me.

Once more, I am extending an offer to you fine sirs to follow the Khuldinai example and serve the Empire. 

As is required of my station, I ask this kindly. I shall not ask again.

*Hammen*  
*Prefect, Imperial Legion First Division*

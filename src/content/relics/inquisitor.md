*These messages were recorded by Scryer Barry, Imperial Legion Third Division, during routine surveillance of the Whisper Wall in the Bastion, Timbur Deep. The letters were sent between senior members of the Empire’s civilian staff and the Temple.*

## A Letter to the Temple

Esteemed Hierophant Draven,

I wish to once again congratulate you on your ascension to the position of Hierophant within the Temple Order. It was my humble honor to submit a statement supporting your petition, and in my time as Councilor I have met no other who was as worthy of such position. I am confident that you will serve the Temple well, and will execute your duties in a manner that is faithful to both the Temple and the people of the Empire.

With that consideration, I wish to bring your attention to a particular matter, which I have previously discussed with your peers. It concerns the Temple’s use of Inquisitors, and of what I fear may reflect a changing attitude among the people. It is my belief that the Inquisitor is an archaic role that has outlived its purpose, and respectfully request the Temple consider dissolution of the position and its duties.

I am reminded that the Pact of 658 CR granted the Temple authority to establish the Inquisitor role as a contingency to root out corruption within the Temple and the Empire. This may have been necessary at the time, however the role was never dissolved as the rebellions were put down. Now, we have an extended period of peace and prosperity within the region, and the Inquisitor continues to surface as a relic of a bygone era.

I have seen the people’s reaction to them firsthand. Even within the Imperial Palace, among Councilors, Merchant House Patrons, and senior Legion Officials, the appearance of an Inquisitor unsettles the masses. It creates the impression that there is a traitor in our midst, even when that is not the case. Thus, I fear, the Inquisitor does more to present the image of instability than stability and negatively impacts how the people view the Temple.

My friend, I have watched your journey from the time you were a Cleric in our Temple through your ascent to Warden of our Domain. I know you to be a man of pure faith and service. I humbly request you consider my request and speak to your fellow Hierophants regarding this proposition.

Regards  
*Councilor Sorren*

## Response from the Temple

Eminent Councilor,

It is good to hear from you again, and I thank you for your kind words. You have been a good friend and mentor during my time in the city, for which I am eternally grateful. No other Councilor has been so kind and welcoming, and I have enjoyed no gatherings as much as I have enjoyed yours. I hope you will see fit to keep me on the guest list and look forward to the next event.

Thank you as well for contacting me regarding your concerns about the Inquisitors. I can assure you that the role remains as necessary today as it did those years ago. As you mentioned, the Pact proved the need for the creation of the position as a direct reporter to the Senary to root out corruption and immorality within the Temple and other organizations. Unfortunately, that mission is as relevant today as it was then, and the Inquisitor serves as a direct connection between the Hierophants and the people.

Furthermore, the Inquisitor role has since expanded to include ensuring the moral teachings of the Temple are upheld within the populace. They are responsible for the administration of the Reclamation program, essential to the future of the Empire. This program has proven difficult to carry out, as any good cause would be, and the progress we have made is a credit to the tireless discipline of the Inquisitors.

I want to reassure you that I have heard your concerns, and I will speak with my fellows in the Senary regarding the matter. I cannot promise you will receive a response in writing, as you know, and ask for your patience and understanding.

Be well my friend.

With Grace  
*Hierophant Draven*

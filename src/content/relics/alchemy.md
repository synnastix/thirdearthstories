*This odd letter features characters written in various colors, or perhaps it was a single color that changed over time.*

Lead into gold?

Lead into gold?!?!

Misguided youth!

Always tinkering. Always dreaming.

It’s good that they dream – it’s the only way to make progress.

But one must eat today!

Eating requires coin. Or something useful in trade. Goods or service.

Learn the craft and make yourself useful!

## Spirits for the Spirit

This is by far the most reliable means of profit, and is where we are most respected. Any brewer can brew, but only an alchemist can distill that brew into something better. An alchemist can extract the spirit of the brew and concentrate it.

Did you know that’s why the drink is called spirits?

Crafty brewers have made strong drink, but the alchemist elevates the drink beyond what the brewer could do. Extracts are stored in barrels and aged. Plum wine becomes brandy. Wheat beer becomes whiskey. We are cooks and alchemists in the same body, feeding the never-ending demand for excess in the city.

Price your art accordingly!

Befriend the brewer or, if you have space, brew your own. Then distill. Then reconstitute to suit your tastes. Rather, suit your client’s taste, as that is more important sometimes than your own. 

### The Carpenter and the Glassmaker

Befriend also the carpenter and the glass blower, for both can exert power over the alchemist. Wooden barrels must be of the correct variety as no one desires cursed spirits. Oak is best, pine is abhorrent. Likewise, the glassmaker’s craft is essential to our own, and their services do not come cheap. The forges, the sand, the skill to make a thing that does not shatter under flame. Pay them well and you will not be underserved.

## The Dressmaker’s Friend

The drab and the dull are the garb of the peasantry. Nobles do not desire such filth.

Befriend the dressmaker, for they have access to deep pockets.

Nobles do not desire the same dreary palette and will pay much for things unique or rare. Silks and quality wool dyed properly capture their attention, and the admiration of those they wish to impress. A simple dye made as follows:

Combine bark of the crab apple with oregano, chopped and dried in the sun. Ferment then boil with wool.

The ease!

A blood-red dye that Sable Rise adores! With lye the shade changes and yet another color is added to the alchemist’s stocks. 

Friends! 

The master tailor needs us and will pay handsomely.

## Scribes and Scribbles

Behold!

The document you have been reading has been written by me with inks that I made myself, using only what was around my humble abode. Scribes need inks too, as artists need paints, and who better to provide for these needs than the alchemist who knows the world’s colors?

I shall tell you a secret:

In the sea, sometimes on the rocks, sometimes in a fisherman’s catch, are creatures that are most grotesque. A bell with snake-like legs, slimy, writhing, and carrying the stench of dead fish. Within them is a sack of ready-made ink, suitable for cloth or scroll. Some even claim it is good for eating though I have not, and shall not, try such a measure myself.

## Divest from Delusion

Friends!

Divest yourselves from fanciful dreams. Focus on the craft that feeds families. Make the lye for the mortar and soaps. Oil for the lamps. Glue. Vinegar. Make the things people want and need.

Leave the experiments of war to the deranged who engage in war and have no regard for their own lives. Leave the potions of love to the witches. Too many promising youth are killed or maimed in pursuit of a Lord’s conquest or noble’s riches. 

Rise above.

Focus on the craft.

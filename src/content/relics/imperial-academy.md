*While the city of Merrow’s Reach remained functionally independent until the year 740 CR, the Academy had been conscripted decades earlier. Originally a gathering of fellows whose sole purpose was the sharing of knowledge, it was brought into service for the Empire as a training center for Legion officers. What follows is a sample of an acceptance where the identities involved have been rendered illegible.*

17th of Reaping, Year 731 CR  

To: *Illegible*
From: Office of Admissions, Imperial Academy of Merrow’s Reach

It is my pleasure to congratulate you on your acceptance into the Imperial Academy of Merrow’s Reach, set to commence at the start of Frostbreak, Year 732 CR.

The Imperial Academy of Merrow’s Reach only accepts the finest candidates from within the Empire. The institution has a storied history and has produced the finest scholars and Legion officers alike. 

This year the Academy reviewed 412 applications for the 12 places available. Your record of service demonstrated an ability to exercise discretion and judgement under significant pressures, distinguishing you among your peers. Your essay on clandestine deposition as an instrument of statehood has been nominated for inclusion during the next curriculum review, an honor that has only been bestowed twice in the Academy’s storied history. In recognition of services rendered to the Empire, it pleases this office to offer you a seat in this year’s cohort. 

At Merrow’s Reach, you will read alongside the noble sons and daughters of the Empire, and participate in activities with the Legion’s future leaders. You will have an opportunity to learn from masters in multiple disciplines, including art, writing, mathematics, and languages. You will progress with your cohort and be held to the same standard as every other student at the Academy, regardless of birthright or commission. Your prior service entitles you to separate living quarters and other benefits, all of which will be explained during your orientation.

Your program has been carefully selected in accordance with your specialized skills and background, and will require approximately eighteen months of careful study to complete. Upon completion of your program, you will return to the Legion, with such rank and responsibility as your training and service merits. Therein, you will be expected to continue serving for a term no less than two months for each month spent at the Academy, or thirty-six months after graduation.

If for any reason you are unable to arrive for the term specified, your command will be notified and the seat will be reassigned to a candidate on the waiting list.

Once again I want to offer my personal congratulations on this award and commend your service to the Empire.

*Illegible*  
Master  
Imperial Academy of Merrow’s Reach
